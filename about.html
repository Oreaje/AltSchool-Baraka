🌧️ The Day the Light Returned

This is a story of hope, resilience, and the power of small acts of kindness that can bring light to the darkest days.

There once was a girl named Amawho felt like the sky had forgotten how to shine for her. Every morning, she woke up with heavy thoughts, like invisible bricks weighing down her heart. People laughed, talked, and lived — but to her, everything felt far away. Quiet. Cold. Colorless. She didn’t know how to say, “I’m hurting,”* because the world kept spinning and she didn’t want to be the one to slow it down. So she hid it. Smiled small. Nodded often. Cried in silence. One rainy evening, she sat under a tree in the schoolyard, watching droplets race each other down a leaf. For a moment, she whispered to the wind, *“Why am I still here?” The wind didn’t answer. But then — something unexpected happened. A little boy from the primary section ran up to her. He held out a crumpled drawing with stick figures and said, “This is you and me. You’re my superhero.” She blinked, confused. “Me?” The boy nodded. “You always smile at me in the hallway. That’s the only time I feel noticed.” In that moment, something cracked open in her chest — not more pain, but warmth. She realized: even her smallest kindness had meaning. Even when she thought she had nothing left to give, someone saw her light. Her existence had value. That night, Ama didn’t magically feel better. But she chose to stay. Chose to try. Chose to believe that healing could come slowly, like a sunrise — quiet, but sure. And one day, the sky began to shine for her again. --- Dear reader, if you feel like Ama, please know: You’re not invisible. You’re not broken beyond repair. Even in your silence, you are loved. Even in your tears, you are strong. Even now — you are worth living for. Please stay.
